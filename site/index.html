    Demand requests now rival new supply in Texas

    Big users have asked the Texas grid for 410 GW. About 87% are data centers (ERCOT, March 2026). New power plants in line add up to 453.6 GW, mostly batteries and solar.

    ERCOT now studies these requests in batches, about every six months. Before, each new request could send earlier projects back to the start.

    Limits

    • City only. The tax list has no addresses, so those sites sit at their city.
    • "Certified" date is when the tax break started, not when the site opened.
    • Tenants as filed. They can differ from the press. The state lists Microsoft at two Red Oak sites, but Google announced a campus there.
    • Firms in the file appear in a permit's emails. Their exact role isn't stated.
    • Small permits are missing. Some generator permits skip the TCEQ list.
    • No MW per site. ERCOT doesn't publish it. Backup generators show size, not actual use.
