    ERCOT's load queue now rivals its generation queue

    ERCOT reported about 410 GW of large loads asking to connect as of March 26, 2026, and said roughly 87% of them are data centers. The generation interconnection queue stood at 453.6 GW on February 28, mostly batteries and solar.

    ERCOT is moving large-load studies to a batch process. Requests are grouped and studied together about every six months, and the capacity each study allocates is reserved. Under the old one-at-a-time process, a new request in the same transmission zone could send earlier projects back into study.

    A request is not a commitment. Many large-load requests are speculative and will not be built.

    Questions I am following

    What the filings raise for each part of the industry. These are open questions, not conclusions.

    Wires utilities

    Five mapped sites sit in a band about ten miles wide south of Dallas, in Lancaster, Wilmer and Red Oak. How will substation and transmission work be sequenced along that corridor?

    Generators and retailers

    A Hill County data center is applying for its own gas turbines and says it will not sell power to the grid. A combined-cycle plant for the grid is filed in the same county. Which loads will buy firm supply, and which will self-supply?

    Data center developers

    With six-month batch studies, the batch a request enters shapes its energization date. How much does time-to-power now drive site choice in DFW?

    Engineering and construction

    One Wilmer application covers 891 standby generators in the DFW ozone nonattainment area. How much do air permitting and generator supply add to the schedule of a large campus?

    Limits

    • City from the name. The Comptroller list gives no address, so those sites are placed at the city in their name.
    • Effective date is when a site's tax certification took effect, not when it was built or energized.
    • Permits by rule and some standard permits are not on the TCEQ pending list, so smaller on-site generation can be missing.
    • No MW per site. ERCOT does not publish project-level large-load data. Generator counts describe backup capacity, not IT load.
